How the auction clears

  1. Supply is released every block. The world's auction sells an equal slice of its tokens in each of its blocks — no one can buy it all in the first second.
  2. Bids say “up to this price”. A bid is an amount of currency and a ceiling. Each block, the clearing price is the lowest price at which demand meets that block's supply.
  3. Everyone pays the same price. Bids above the clearing price are filled at the clearing price, never at their ceiling; the difference is refunded when the bid exits.
  4. Graduation opens a Uniswap v4 pool. When the auction ends having raised enough, Uniswap's LBPStrategy opens a pool at the discovered price, paired with the parent world's token, under the lineage hook that pays royalties up the family line.

Bid without a wallet

In the UNMAPPED app: Worlds → Market, pick the world, enter an amount and approve Touch ID. The app turns the signature into a relayed transaction; the player never holds ETH or connects a wallet.

After the auction

The pool opens when the auction is settled.
